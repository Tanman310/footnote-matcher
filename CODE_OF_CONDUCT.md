# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Footnote Matcher is a small project made by volunteers. We want taking part in it to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their background, identity, or level of experience. Plenty of contributors here are new to open source, and that's welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect people's time: write clear issues and keep discussions on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive behavior after being asked to stop

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit messages) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report problems to the maintainer at **footnote-matcher@miller310.com**. Reports are kept confidential. You can also use GitHub's **Report content** option on any comment.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that break this code. Depending on the severity, the response may be a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project.
